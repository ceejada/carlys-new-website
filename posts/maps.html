Public Transit in Rochester from a Geospatial Perspective

For my final project in my Mapping & Geospatial Technologies class, I created an interactive StoryMap exploring public transit in Rochester, New York.

Over my time in college, I've taken three courses in geographic information systems and mapping. What started as a way to branch out from my major specific courses quickly turned into something I really enjoyed! I’ve always loved geography and maps, and these classes gave me the opportunity to learn some useful technical skills and practice design and data analysis.

A StoryMap is a common deliverable in GIS classes, as it combines spatial data, analysis, and narrative into one format. My final in IGME-382, or Maps Mapping and Geospatial Technologies, was an analysis on public transportation in Rochester, New York. Rochester has been my home for the past four years and I have always been both fascinated and frustrated by certain parts of how the city functions. I’m also a bit of a public transportation enthusiast, so this project felt like the perfect opportunity to explore those interests. So I made a StoryMap about it, check it out below :)
