Dinosaur Chair

this is a sample blog post to make sure everything works right, featuring a high school woodworking project!

i made this in high school but i still think about it sometimes this was so much fun. i’d love to do more like this sometime soon! sandy, eliana, and i designed the dino chair, modeled it in fusion, assembled and tweaked some laser cut models, and then manufactured and polished the chair in the shop! i got to play with design, 3d modeling and printing, and jig saws, what more could you ask for? i’d love to get a chance to build more stuff with my hands some time soon
