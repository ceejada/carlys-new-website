Dinosaur Chair

This is a sample blog post to make sure everything works right, featuring a high school woodworking project!

I made this in high school but I still think about it sometimes, this was a really fun project! Sandy, Eliana, and I designed the dino chair, modeled it in Fusion, assembled and tweaked some laser cut models, and then manufactured and polished the chair in the shop! I got to play with design, 3D modeling and printing, and jigsaw — what more could you ask for? I’d love to get a chance to build more stuff with my hands sometime soon :)
